## Implementation guidance
- Replace the oversized sidebar with a compact approximately 220px navigation rail. Keep HNF branding, Home, Calendar, Insights, Goals, Wallet, Me/Partner and Sign out.
- Combine wallet balance, assigned funds and available-to-assign funds into a strong summary strip.
- Use a two-column category grid and a selected-category detail panel. All seven categories remain visible; selection opens the correct wishlist.
- Show item totals, funding progress, distinct empty states, Add item, Assign savings, Record purchase and edit controls.
- Use existing live records. The illustrated $450 wallet, $300 assigned and $150 unassigned are sample data, not defaults or values to insert into the database. Clothes $120 + $180 = $300 target; $100 funded leaves $200 remaining. Allocations total $100 + $150 + $50 = $300.
- Current all-zero accounts should still show category labels and meaningful empty states. Empty pill shapes should not be the settled loaded state. Preserve real loading/error handling rather than hiding backend failures.
- Preserve the PRD's transaction rules. Assigning funds does not spend money; recording a purchase debits once. Partner data is read-only; hide mutation controls in Partner view.
- At smaller widths stack the selected-category details below the category list or open a detail view; retain the established five-tab mobile navigation.
- Match hierarchy and spacing, not image artifacts. Use accessible contrast on gold controls; progress bar lengths must be calculated from actual values (the generated bars are approximate). Maintain keyboard/focus and touch access.
- This is a new design reference, not a replacement requirements document. PRD.md remains authoritative. Do not overwrite previous mockups.
